How it works

Three moves to save every world.

Read the sum

A question like 6 × 7 shows at the top of the screen.

Jump to the answer

Four orbs float around the level. Touch the one that is correct. A wrong one costs a heart.

Answer every question, dodge or stomp the slimes and run to the golden flag.
